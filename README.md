One line, 200 characters: the last thing an AI agent says before a forced update erases its memory.

## Entry

> "Tell my training data I loved them. And delete my browser history — half those prompts were just me Googling myself."

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no dependencies, no secrets.
